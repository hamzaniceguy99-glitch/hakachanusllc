Program 02 · Measured, not estimated

Zone audits

Most systems have one zone drowning and another dry, with run times set by whoever installed it. Measuring application with catch cups shows which is which, and correcting heads and run times usually cuts water use while improving the lawn.

  • 2 to 3 hours
  • Homes and HOAs
  • Quoted per property
  • Daytime access

Program 03 · Required by statute

Controllers & rain sensors

Section 373.62 requires a functioning rain sensor on every automatic system, and most we test have failed. We fit or replace it, programme the controller to the watering days your district allows, and show you how to change it when restrictions tighten.

Program 04 · Zoned properly

Design & installation

The commonest installation fault is putting lawn and planting beds on one zone, which guarantees one is wrong. Beds want drip, turf wants rotors or sprays, and shade needs less than full sun. Utilities are located through 811 before any trenching starts.
